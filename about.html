About Moore Bonsai

Character, health & patience.

Moore Bonsai focuses on sourcing, caring for and developing bonsai trees with character and long-term potential.

Each tree is assessed as an individual. The goal is not simply to sell a plant, but to place a living piece of art with someone who will enjoy its development.

From acquisition to new home

We look at health, structure, movement, bark, root condition and future potential when selecting trees. Once acquired, each tree is cared for through the seasons before being offered for sale.
