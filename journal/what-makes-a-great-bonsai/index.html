What Makes a Great Bonsai?

2026-07-20

Looking beyond age to movement, taper, bark and future potential.

A great bonsai is not simply an old tree. Structure, health, movement, taper, bark and the potential for future development all matter.

Movement gives a tree character. Taper helps create the impression of age, while good branch placement creates a believable miniature tree.

Perhaps most importantly, a good bonsai should be healthy. A beautiful structure is only useful when the tree has the vigour to support its continued development.
