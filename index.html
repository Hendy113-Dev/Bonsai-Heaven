The Moore Bonsai approach

Good bonsai takes time.

We believe the best trees are not rushed. From sourcing and initial assessment to pruning, feeding, watering and seasonal work, every tree receives thoughtful attention.

Our aim is simple: to place healthy, characterful bonsai with people who will enjoy developing them for years to come.
