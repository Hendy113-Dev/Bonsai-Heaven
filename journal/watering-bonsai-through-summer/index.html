Watering Bonsai Through Summer

2026-08-01

A practical guide to keeping bonsai healthy during warm weather.

Summer watering is about observation rather than a rigid timetable. Check the compost and the tree, water thoroughly when needed, and make sure excess water can drain freely.

Small pots can dry surprisingly quickly during warm or windy weather. Check individual trees rather than watering everything on the same schedule.

Morning watering is often convenient, but the important thing is that the tree receives the water it needs and is not left repeatedly bone dry.
